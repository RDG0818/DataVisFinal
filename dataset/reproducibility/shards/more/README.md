# More-schools CDS shard

This shard supplies 377 observations across 30 school-years from 13 schools in `observations.csv`; 28 school-years from 12 schools have `source_table_reviewed` C1 totals. Two Penn State University Park rows are held separately at `indexed_table_reviewed_page_unverified` because the official PDF search index exposed a complete C1 table but the live PDF server returned HTTP 502 and its physical page could not be checked. The integration should exclude those rows until the binary or rendered PDF is independently reviewed.

`extract_reviewed.py` records manual transcriptions of university-hosted CDS tables. `sources.csv` gives one official URL per school-year, and `../../sources/more/*.txt` retains the transcribed C1 triple, page, and notes. The university PDFs could be read through an official-source web renderer, but direct binary download from those hosts failed TLS in this environment. Therefore `local_path` and SHA-256 are intentionally blank. The web renderer's `P0` is recorded as physical page 1.

The included 12 reviewed schools are USC, Carnegie Mellon, Johns Hopkins, Rice, Emory, Boston College, Tufts, Boston University, Florida, Rochester, Caltech, and Northeastern. Fall 2023 C1 is present for all except Rochester, enabling the root project's 2023 IPEDS comparison; Fall 2024 C1 is present for all 12. USC adds 2020–2022, and Carnegie Mellon and Emory add 2025. The USC 2019–2020 archive HTML has blank C1 fields while its PDF says Fall 2018; no Fall 2019 observation was inferred from that edition.

Fall 2024 C9 submit rates and SAT/ACT composite percentile triples, plus SAT reading/writing and math triples, are present for Carnegie Mellon, Johns Hopkins, Rice, Emory, Boston College, Tufts, Florida, and Northeastern. Rochester's 2024 C9 table explicitly applies only to Arts, Sciences & Engineering while its C1 is institution-wide including Eastman; those C9 values were omitted to avoid mixing populations.

`aid_need_based_received_count` is H2-D (any financial aid among students with need), while `aid_need_based_grant_received_count` is H2-E (recipients of a need-based scholarship or grant). Northeastern and Emory H2 data use the first-time, full-time first-year population. Their reported H2 values are **estimated aid-year values**, as the sources state. GPA is on the reported 4-point scale where reported. `undergraduate_enrollment` is B1 total all undergraduates, including non-degree students when the institution includes them. C9 percentiles represent score submitters, not all enrolled freshmen.

Not every university publishes every optional field, and this is a deliberately sparse panel. C7 factor importance was not transcribed because its categories are not yet in the shared numeric metric schema. Some school reports have campus scope different from an IPEDS UNITID (notably Carnegie Mellon Pittsburgh only, UF Main Campus, Penn State University Park, and potential Emory/Oxford treatment). Preserve any IPEDS disagreement in `consistency.csv`; do not replace the official CDS value. The Emory 2023 official C1 reports 33,255 applicants on physical page 7, while the root project's ADM2023 export reports 31,929; both figures need to remain visible.

The `source_table_reviewed` label means table rows and context were inspected in the official web-rendered document or HTML. It does not claim a locally downloaded PDF hash or an independent second transcriber. No numbers were filled from IPEDS or third-party compilations.
