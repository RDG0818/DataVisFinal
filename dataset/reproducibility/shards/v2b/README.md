# v2b official CDS additions

`python3 shards/v2b/build.py` regenerates this shard. It currently includes 10 institutions, 15 institution-years, and 158 reviewed numeric observations. Every accepted institution-year has an official CDS C1 applicant/admit/enrollee triple. Blank cells and unpublished editions are omitted, never encoded as zero. `observations.csv` retains the URL and the physical one-based PDF page (or workbook sheet/cell). `sources.csv` records the retrieved binary hash when a local official source was accessible.

The source URLs are university-hosted PDFs or XLSX documents, including university-owned Box shares linked from institutional reporting pages. University of Illinois 2023/2024, Purdue 2023/2024, UT Austin 2023/2024, Wisconsin Madison 2023/2024, UW Seattle 2023/2024, UMD College Park 2023/2024, and Ohio State 2024 official binaries are under `raw/v2b/`; extracted UT/Wisconsin text is under `sources/v2b/`. Ohio State 2023, Georgia Tech 2023/2024, Rutgers 2023, UMass 2023, Texas A&M 2023, and Minnesota 2024 were read in official web-rendered PDFs, so no local SHA-256 is claimed. `recovery_sources.csv` lists additional official files for root's Kaggle CPU document recovery; inferred Maryland 2024 URL and formerly indexed Virginia Tech 2023 URL must be checked before use.

Purdue 2024-25 has a source inconsistency: its printed admitted total of 30,272 conflicts with both the gender and residency category sums of 39,272. The primary `admitted` metric preserves 30,272; the separate `admitted_sum_of_reported_categories` metric records 39,272 as a derived alternative for editorial review. Purdue 2023-24 CDS enrollee count differs from IPEDS and is preserved as published. Do not merge Purdue 2024-25 with a West Lafayette-only trend without flagging the university's documented coverage change: it includes West Lafayette, Indianapolis, and Polytechnic Statewide, while prior CDS years covered West Lafayette only. Minnesota Twin Cities 2023 C1 was deliberately withheld because the official PDF's gender and residency totals conflict; no arbitrary selection was made. Texas Austin 2024 C2 has blank counts, so no waitlist observations were recorded. A `C1` triple is the acceptance gate; no IPEDS fields were used to fill CDS gaps. C9 submission percentages refer to enrolled students, whereas C1 rates are computed from applicant/admit/enrollee counts.

UW Seattle 2024 and Ohio State 2024 PDFs are form-filled: ordinary text extraction lost the response numbers. Their C1/C2 figures were reviewed on the rendered source pages 13-14. Ohio State 2024 has an explicit C2 offered value of zero and blank accepted/admitted cells; the latter remain omitted. UMD College Park 2023/2024 has explicit zero waitlist accepted/admitted values, which are retained. Auburn 2023/2024 is official institutional research HTML; its source `format` is `html`.

NC State 2023/2024 and Clemson 2023/2024 official direct URLs remain candidates in `recovery_sources.csv`. The final private Kaggle CPU recovery attempt for these four files was rejected before kernel creation because the account had reached its maximum of five batch CPU sessions. No unreviewed values from these candidates were added. Penn State University Park 2023/2024 downloads failed with RemoteDisconnected; Virginia Tech 2023 URL returned 404; neither was filled from third-party numbers.
